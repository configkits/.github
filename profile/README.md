## ` What is ConfigKits? `

> *We're a small software organisation that builds three kinds of things — and we build all three seriously.*

ConfigKits sits at the intersection of **open source craft**, **product thinking**, and **client delivery**. We don't just ship code. We ship things that are genuinely usable, maintainable, and worth someone's time.

## ` Open Source Philosophy `

Our open-source work follows a simple contract:

- **Usable** — works out of the box, not just in theory
- **Documented** — READMEs you can actually follow
- **Maintained** — issues get responses, PRs get reviewed
- **Honest** — we say what it does and what it doesn't

## ` Contribute `

All public repos welcome contributions. Here's how:

1. **Find something broken or missing** — open an issue first
2. **Fork → branch → fix** — keep PRs focused and small
3. **Follow the repo's CONTRIBUTING.md** — each project has one
4. **Write tests if they exist** — don't break what's working

We review PRs within a few days. We give real feedback, not just "LGTM".
